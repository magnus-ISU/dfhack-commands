[NAME:HA - Existing Civilizations Playable]
[DESCRIPTION:Part of the Dwarf Fortress: High Adventure suite. Standalone fork of All Races Playable Redo: play dwarves, humans, elves, or goblins in fortress mode, and any of them plus kobolds as adventure-mode outsiders. Goblins additionally get bronze-working and a single autumn caravan season (High Adventure evil trade bloc). Kobold forts are NOT included here - vanilla kobolds embark broken (citizens appear as livestock); the ha-kobolds mod supplies the playable kobold civilization instead. See CHANGES.md for the full diff from the source mod.]
