Dummy creature used only as a 1-tick body-transformation waypoint for caste changes
(illithid -> ulitharid -> elder brain). The neural-bath script sets a unit's identity
caches to the target caste, then applies the HA_ILLITHID_TF syndrome which turns the unit
into this flux for a single tick; when it reverts, DF rebuilds the unit's body, appearance
vectors and graphics from the caches, landing cleanly on the target caste. This is the
succubus-mod pattern: transforming the caste directly (setting unit.caste) leaves the
appearance vectors sized for the old caste and CRASHES the game when the description is
opened. Never invoked in worldgen; exists purely as a transform target.
