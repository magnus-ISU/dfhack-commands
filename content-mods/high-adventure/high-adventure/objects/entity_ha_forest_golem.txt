entity_ha_forest_golem

[OBJECT:ENTITY]

Forest golems: a civilization that founds ONE forest retreat and never founds
another. The placement layout is the same one ha-high-elves uses -- see the
comments there -- and the reasoning bears repeating: BIOME_SUPPORT is not what
limits expansion. SETTLEMENT_BIOME and START_BIOME are what grant the right to
build, so both are absent; EXCLUSIVE_START_BIOME places the first site and
BIOME_SUPPORT stays only because worldgen rejects a world with none at all.

Because each civ is exactly one grove, MAX_STARTING_CIV_NUMBER is the dial for
"how many golem groves exist in the world". They start in forest, which is where
the elves start, so the two compete for the same handful of good sites.

They are also the quietest civ in the suite, on purpose. The four tokens that
send members out into the world as roaming historical figures -- WANDERER,
BEAST_HUNTER, SCOUT, MERCENARY -- are all absent, which is how vanilla kobolds
stay out of everyone's history. NO_ARTIFACT_CLAIMS keeps them out of artifact
quest chains, and they carry none of SIEGER, AMBUSHER, BABYSNATCHER, ITEM_THIEF
or LOCAL_BANDITRY, so they never mount an offensive. What they cannot be stopped
from doing is holding professions and being drawn into wars -- no token exists
for either.
