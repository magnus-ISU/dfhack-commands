creature_ha_mephit

[OBJECT:CREATURE]

Mephits: minor elemental spirits, bound out of a gem at the Gargoyle Forge. Three castes here
-- air, acid, ice. The fourth sibling is the VANILLA fire imp, which already carries exactly
the right kit (FIREIMMUNE, MAGMA_VISION, fireball at 15 tiles, firejet at 5) and so costs us no
new creature; warlocks.lua mints one when the forge's binding happens to roll fire.

Constructs, not animals: no MALE/FEMALE, no CHILD, so they never breed, and NO_EAT/NO_DRINK/
NO_SLEEP so they never cost a thing to keep. Each caste emits its element at 5 tiles on a
300-tick cooldown, which is the pacing the original used.
