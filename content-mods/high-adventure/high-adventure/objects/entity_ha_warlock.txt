entity_ha_warlock

[OBJECT:ENTITY]

The warlock civilization. Fortress-playable; adventure mode is not wired up in 0.1.

NOTE what is missing on purpose: there is no MAYOR position, and the Sorcerer Supreme carries
no REPLACED_BY. In vanilla the expedition leader is replaced by an elected mayor at fifty
citizens; defining one here would displace the Sorcerer Supreme and leave the fort with no
auto-appointed noble able to appoint the Horsemen.

(Tokens are never written inside these comments: DF parses any bracketed token it finds,
comment or not, so a token named in prose would take effect.)

Migrants are suppressed by warlocks.lua, not here -- no raw token disables them.

	Placement and expansion, following the pattern ha-forest-golems and ha-high-elves already
	use: EXCLUSIVE_START_BIOME with NO SETTLEMENT_BIOME and NO START_BIOME. That combination
	founds one settlement and no more, because SETTLEMENT_BIOME and START_BIOME are what grant
	a civ the right to build, and neither is here. There is no token that says "do not expand";
	withholding the right to build is the mechanism.

	BIOME_SUPPORT is NOT the limiter -- its numbers are relative to each other only -- but it
	cannot be dropped either: with no BIOME_SUPPORT at all worldgen rejects every candidate
	world. The three weights below are the human ones for the biomes humans start in.

	Where humans go: vanilla PLAINS starts in ANY_GRASSLAND, ANY_SAVANNA and ANY_SHRUBLAND.
	EXCLUSIVE_START_BIOME takes a single biome everywhere it is used (vanilla uses it once, for
	dwarves; both HA mods above use one), so warlocks start in the grassland and the other two
	appear only as support. Whether several EXCLUSIVE_START_BIOME entries are legal is untested.
