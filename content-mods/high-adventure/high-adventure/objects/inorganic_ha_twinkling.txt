Twinkling metal -- High Adventure's fixed "divine metal" for the high elves.

Material PROPERTIES are cloned from vanilla's generated divine metal
(vanilla_procedural/scripts/generators/divine.lua, materials.divine.metal.default)
-- crucially MELTING_POINT:NONE and BOILING_POINT:NONE, which is what makes
divine metal truly dragonfire-safe (it cannot melt at any temperature; adamantine
melts at 25000 and is NOT dragonfire-safe). Same strength/density/edge as divine
metal, so twinkling gear is light, effective, and indestructible by fire.

We then add adamantine's THREAD-metal flags (ITEMS_SOFT, WAFERS,
STOCKPILE_THREAD_METAL) so it can also be woven into cloth and processed into
bars/wafers -- vanilla divine metal is metal-only; we want a divine metal that is
ALSO a fabric, so twinkling cloaks are near-armour AND fireproof.

Name/colour = the STARS-sphere divine metal actually named "twinkling metal" in
divine.lua. It is grown at the Shaping Tree ("catch starlight"), never mined, so
no DEEP_SPECIAL.

Both twinkling materials carry the SPECIAL token (written in brackets in the
definitions below, never in this prose -- see the warning in
reaction_ha_high_elf.txt). SPECIAL stops AI-controlled entities making items from
the material and stops it travelling in caravans; the player uses it exactly as
normal, so a high-elf fort still catches starlight and forges bars. That is the
division of labour this suite already runs on: elves get their twinkling gear from
war-gear.lua, which hands it to them directly, so worldgen's equipment spread has no
business minting it and an elf caravan has no business hauling divine fireproof
cloaks to a dwarf fort in exchange for ale. Adamantine gets the same protection
implicitly -- DEEP_SPECIAL implies SPECIAL -- which is why AI civs never trade it.

TWINKLING FABRIC: what a caught strand becomes if it is WOVEN instead of smelted.
Same divine stuff as the metal, but soft: only the soft-item tokens are present, so
DF will make thread, cloth and clothing from it and nothing else. Deliberately NOT
ITEMS_HARD/ITEMS_METAL/ITEMS_ARMOR/ITEMS_WEAPON -- armour and weapons come from the
metal bar, which costs a strand plus silver at a smelter.

Built on METAL_TEMPLATE for the same reason vanilla adamantine cloth is: that is the
proven path for a mineral strand becoming thread, then cloth, then worn clothing. It
keeps the divine strength values, which is the point of a divine fabric.
