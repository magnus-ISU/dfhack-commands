creature_ha_gibberling

Gibberlings: blue, chitin-plated gibbering predators. Redesigned from Highfantasy's
feral goblin toward the canonical Cacame gibberling body - chitin exoskeleton,
whipping tail, hooked claws, NO hands, ichor blood - while keeping our blue theme,
cannibalism, and skill-tiered castes. Intelligent (CAN_LEARN/CAN_SPEAK) but their
speech is only mad babble (UTTERANCES). Self-contained against vanilla.
Named for the gibberlings that slew the parents of Cacame Awemedinade.
