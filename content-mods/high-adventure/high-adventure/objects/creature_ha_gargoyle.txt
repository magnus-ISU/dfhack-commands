creature_ha_gargoyle

[OBJECT:CREATURE]

Gargoyles: statues cut at the Gargoyle Forge out of fifteen boulders and given a bound soul.
Built on vanilla's elemental-man construct idiom (HUMANOID_SIMPLE + a single stone TISSUE_LAYER
+ ITEMCORPSE:STATUE), because that shape is known to work.

[IMMOBILE] is the whole point and also the problem: an immobile creature can never walk to the
pasture you assign it. warlocks.lua teleports each gargoyle into its assigned pasture after a
delay, while nobody is looking at it.

Three castes:
	MELEE  fifteen boulders, nothing else. Punches. The free early defence.
	FIRE   + fifteen souls. Spits a jet of fire, five tiles.
	ICE    + fifteen souls and a gem. Spits a freezing shard, five tiles.
