[OBJECT:CREATURE]

High elf: a full standalone copy of the vanilla ELF creature (every tag spelled
out, not COPY_TAGS_FROM, so we can freely edit body/tissue/caste tokens and add
graphics conditions later). Only the identity differs -- name, description, tile
colour (white). Same body, castes, tissues (hair/skin/eye TL_COLOR_MODIFIERs kept
so graphics palettes have something to key off), attributes and lifespan as elves.
No strange moods -- moods are a dwarf-race behaviour, so an elf-race creature
never gets them. Graphics are TODO (reuse elf sprites / workshop art off-blue);
until then they fall back to this tile.
