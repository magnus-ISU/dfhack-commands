reaction_ha_warlock

[OBJECT:REACTION]

Reactions that make a unit produce NOTHING here. warlocks.lua watches JOB_COMPLETED and
creates the creature; that is also where the caps live, since a reaction cannot count what
already exists. Jobs are cancelled at ASSIGNMENT time when a cap or condition fails, so the
souls are never spent on a refusal.

Every gem line ships twice -- once for a real gemstone, once for cut clear glass -- because
glass is not IS_GEM and an ANY_GEM_MATERIAL reagent silently rejects it.

WORTHLESS_STONE_ONLY on the gargoyle reagents is deliberate: the original mod's forge happily
ate fifteen hematite boulders and platinum nuggets. Ours will not touch ore or economic stone.

======================================== LIAISON'S OFFICE ======================================

A summoned caravan is a timed_event written straight into df.global.timed_events. The script
no-ops (and says so) when that civ already has a caravan outstanding, because DF then schedules
none of its own -- see dfhack/fort/caravan-unstick.lua for what that failure looks like.

[REACTION:HA_LURE_CARAVAN_OWN]
	[NAME:a. Lure a warlock caravan (5 souls)]
	[BUILDING:HA_LIAISONS_OFFICE:CUSTOM_A]
	[REAGENT:soul:5:BOULDER:NONE:INORGANIC:HA_SOUL]
	[SKILL:NEGOTIATION]

[REACTION:HA_LURE_CARAVAN_GOBLIN]
	[NAME:b. Lure a goblin caravan (10 souls)]
	[BUILDING:HA_LIAISONS_OFFICE:CUSTOM_B]
	[REAGENT:soul:10:BOULDER:NONE:INORGANIC:HA_SOUL]
	[SKILL:NEGOTIATION]

[REACTION:HA_LURE_CARAVAN_ANY]
	[NAME:c. Lure any neighbour's caravan (10 souls)]
	[BUILDING:HA_LIAISONS_OFFICE:CUSTOM_C]
	[REAGENT:soul:10:BOULDER:NONE:INORGANIC:HA_SOUL]
	[SKILL:NEGOTIATION]
