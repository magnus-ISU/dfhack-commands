[OBJECT:CREATURE]

The wild Ancient Dragon: a solitary MEGABEAST (DIFFICULTY:18), NOT part of any kobold civ and NOT
a POWER. Same design as the HA_KOBOLD ANCIENT_DRAGON caste -- asexual, four legs + two wings + a
tail (with a tail-slam), iron-hard scales, dragonfire, flight, legendary combat, phys 2000-3000,
size of a full-grown dragon at 100 and a giant elephant at 1000, immortal like an elf (no MAXAGE).
Based on the vanilla dragon.
