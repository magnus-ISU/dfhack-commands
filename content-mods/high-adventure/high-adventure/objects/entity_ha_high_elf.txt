	land holders both, and build CAVE_DETAILED. High elves build towns, so it should

	reference that founds one settlement and no more. Territory spread is NOT the
	limiter: BIOME_SUPPORT numbers are relative to each other only, so a lone entry at
	weight 1 still puts the entire expansion budget into forest. What actually grants
	the right to build is SETTLEMENT_BIOME and START_BIOME, so those are gone.
	BIOME_SUPPORT has to stay: with none at all worldgen rejects every candidate world.
	[EXCLUSIVE_START_BIOME:ANY_FOREST]
