building_ha_warlock

[OBJECT:BUILDING]

Every building here is warlock-only work: warlocks.lua pins each workshop's
permitted_workers to a warlock, because reactions cannot be caste-gated in raws.
The Obsidian Factory is no exception -- summoning stone out of nothing is magic too.

BUILD_ITEMs are NOT consumed: DF parks them in the building and hands them back when you
deconstruct it. The shrines' gold and books are therefore a deposit, not a price.
