[OBJECT:GRAPHICS]

Elder brain: the illithid 3x2 sprite. Defined as a LAYERED graphic (LAYER_SET +
LAYER), NOT a simple a plain DEFAULT layer with LARGE_IMAGE. DF renders a creature in
grayscale whenever it flashes it (status/legendary indicator) or it is
underworld/underwater -- creatures drawn with LAYERED graphics are EXEMPT and
stay in colour, simple large sprites are not, which is why it flickered
gray<->colour. One unconditioned body layer over the pre-coloured 3x2 image.
