[OBJECT:GRAPHICS]

Forest golem: the bronze colossus 3x2 sprite recoloured iron-gray. It MUST be
a LAYERED graphic (LAYER_SET + LAYER), not a simple DEFAULT with LARGE_IMAGE:
DF renders a creature in grayscale whenever it flashes it (status/legendary
indicator) or it is underwater, but LAYERED graphics are exempt and stay in
colour -- a simple large sprite flickers. Same fix as the drider.

Deliberately NO item layers (no CONDITION_ITEM_WORN / WIELD): worn clothing,
armor and weapons are only drawn where the graphic defines a layer for them,
and humanoid item layers would land at random spots on the 3x2 sprite.
