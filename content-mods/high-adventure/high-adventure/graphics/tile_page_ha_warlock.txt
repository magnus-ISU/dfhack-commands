[OBJECT:TILE_PAGE]

One 8x2 page of 32x32 map sprites, cut from the ☼Warlocks☼ cheat sheet posted in bay12 thread
175304 (the mod's own release is gone; see content-mods/warlocks/README.md). The sheet is 1:1
pixel art on a 32 px grid for the civ, prisoner and mephit blocks, so those sprites are native
and untouched; the gargoyle and golem blocks are drawn smaller on the sheet, so those three
were trimmed to their own bounds and centred in a 32 px cell rather than upscaled.

Attribution for this art is Meph, Vordak, Redshrike and Denzi -- see LICENSE.md section 3,
where the CC-BY-SA question is recorded and still unresolved.

PAGE_DIM_PIXELS must match the PNG exactly. It is 256x64. Getting this wrong makes the
compositor read out of bounds and corrupt the heap, not just draw the wrong tile.
