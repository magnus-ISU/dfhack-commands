[OBJECT:GRAPHICS]

Drider: the drow spider-caste 3x2 sprite. It MUST be a LAYERED graphic
(LAYER_SET + LAYER), NOT a simple a plain DEFAULT layer with LARGE_IMAGE. DF renders a
creature in grayscale whenever the game flashes it (status/legendary indicator)
or it is underwater/underworld -- but creatures drawn with LAYERED graphics are
EXEMPT and stay in colour. A simple large sprite is not exempt, so it flickered
gray<->colour every flash. One unconditioned body layer over the pre-coloured
3x2 image is all that's needed. Large portraits fall through to the drow file.

Deliberately NO item layers (no CONDITION_ITEM_WORN / WIELD): worn clothing,
armor and weapons are only drawn where the graphic defines a layer for them, so
with none here nothing gets overlaid on the drider's spider body. Do NOT add
humanoid item layers -- they would land at random spots on the 3x2 sprite. (The
base HA_DROW item layers are all gated to the FEMALE/MALE castes and never match
a DRIDER-caste unit anyway.)
