graphics_ha_ancient_dragon_portrait

[OBJECT:GRAPHICS]

Portrait for the wild megabeast. Kept in its own file and its own CREATURE_GRAPHICS block, the
way vanilla splits DRAGON across graphics_creatures_megabeast.txt (DEFAULT) and
graphics_creatures_portraits.txt (PORTRAIT) -- separate blocks for the same creature are
additive as long as each one defines a DIFFERENT layer set.

The map sprite is a palette-recoloured composite, but the portrait is a single flat tile: that
is what vanilla dragons get too, and the palette rows only line up with Naut's sprite regions,
not with hand-drawn portrait art.
