[NAME:HA - Adventure Hostility]
[DESCRIPTION:Part of the Dwarf Fortress: High Adventure suite. A DFHack behavior component (no creatures/items): in ADVENTURE mode it forces designated civilizations hostile to the adventurer - and ONLY the adventurer, not their own people - by putting nearby members into a conflict with you. Loners (orcs, mind flayers) attack any non-kin; the evil bloc (drow, dark dwarves, goblins, succubi) attacks any non-bloc adventurer; good civs (humans, elves) attack any evil adventurer but can yield; militant good (high elves, dwarves) attack the same evil set but never break or yield; the kobold civ (and its ancient-dragon overlords) attacks any non-kobold. The evil set is orc, kobold, goblin, succubus, dark dwarf, drow and mind flayer. Hostile civs will not yield unless you are a skilled enough Pacifier (per-race threshold), and the always-hostile evil civs never flee (NOFEAR). Kobolds are the exception on both counts, because the race is cowardly by nature: they stand and fight only while one of their ancient-dragon overlords is on screen, and break and run as soon as none is. The same dragon decides how hard they are to talk down - a kobold yields at Pacify 1 alone but 12 under an overlord, and an overlord itself always needs 12 (the standalone ancient-dragon megabeast is untouched). Inside a settlement a creature follows the ETHICS OF THE TOWN instead of its own race: everyone in an orc town fights like an orc, so a kobold or elf living among orcs comes at you with them, while an orc in a human town is used to being around people and leaves you alone. Out in the wilderness, on ground nobody owns, and for an invading army anywhere, each creature falls back on its own race. What the town never decides is what a creature IS - whether it breaks and how hard it is to talk down stay keyed to its actual race. Races not present in the world are ignored. Auto-enables in adventure mode when this mod is active. See adventure-hostility.lua.]
