[NAME:HA - Adventure Hostility]
[DESCRIPTION:Part of the Dwarf Fortress: High Adventure suite. A DFHack behavior component (no creatures/items): in ADVENTURE mode it forces designated civilizations hostile to the adventurer - and ONLY the adventurer, not their own people - by putting nearby members into a conflict with you. Loners (orcs, mind flayers) attack any non-kin; the evil bloc (drow, dark dwarves, goblins, succubi) attacks any non-bloc adventurer; good civs attack evil adventurers. Hostile civs will not yield unless you are a skilled enough Pacifier (per-race threshold), and the always-hostile evil civs never flee (NOFEAR). Races not present in the world are ignored. Auto-enables in adventure mode when this mod is active. See adventure-hostility.lua.]
