[NAME:HA - Adventure Hostility]
[DESCRIPTION:Part of the Dwarf Fortress: High Adventure suite. A DFHack behavior component (no creatures/items): in ADVENTURE mode it forces designated civilizations hostile to the adventurer - and ONLY the adventurer, not their own people - by putting nearby members into a conflict with you. Loners (orcs, mind flayers) attack any non-kin; the evil bloc (drow, dark dwarves, goblins, succubi) attacks any non-bloc adventurer; good civs (humans, elves) attack any evil adventurer but can yield; militant good (high elves, dwarves) attack the same evil set but never break or yield; the kobold civ (and its ancient-dragon overlords) attacks any non-kobold. The evil set is orc, kobold, goblin, succubus, dark dwarf, drow and mind flayer. Hostile civs will not yield unless you are a skilled enough Pacifier (per-race threshold), and the always-hostile evil civs never flee (NOFEAR). Kobolds yield at Pacify 1, but 12 while a dragon overlord is on screen; a dragon overlord needs 12 (the standalone ancient-dragon megabeast is untouched). Races not present in the world are ignored. Auto-enables in adventure mode when this mod is active. See adventure-hostility.lua.]
