[NAME:HA - Drow]
[DESCRIPTION:Part of the Dwarf Fortress: High Adventure suite. Adds the drow: evil, matriarchal elves in insular dwarven-style mountain fortresses that never expand, hostile to all outsiders. They favor scimitars and bows but also carry scourges, whips, long swords, and picks, use battle axes to fell trees, wear no shields, forge any metal, and are always accompanied by domesticated giant cave spiders. Ruled by queens and high priestesses; their militias are led by honored males. Four percent of drow are born driders - a drow lady's torso on a chitin-plated giant spider body, with paralyzing venom and webs. Fully playable in fortress mode and adventure mode. See CHANGES.md.]
