entity_ha_increased_banditry

[OBJECT:ENTITY]

Raises bandit-group generation for the two vanilla base civilizations, kept out of
the playable-civs component so it can be toggled independently. SELECT_ENTITY
appends these tokens to the existing vanilla entities; the later BANDITRY value
overrides the vanilla one at worldgen.

	Humans: vanilla BANDITRY:10 -> 30 (no LOCAL_BANDITRY, matching vanilla scope)
[SELECT_ENTITY:PLAINS]
	[BANDITRY:30]

	Elves: vanilla has no banditry -> 20, confined to their own territory
[SELECT_ENTITY:FOREST]
	[BANDITRY:20]
	[LOCAL_BANDITRY]
