[NAME:High Adventure - Increased Banditry]
[DESCRIPTION:Part of the Dwarf Fortress: High Adventure suite. Raises the rate at which the two vanilla base civilizations throw off bandit groups - Humans to 30, Elves to 20 (confined to their own lands) - via SELECT_ENTITY so the change stays separate from the playable-civs component and can be toggled on its own at worldgen. Does not touch any of the suite's own civilizations (their banditry is set in their own mods). Raws only; affects worldgen. See CHANGES.md.]
