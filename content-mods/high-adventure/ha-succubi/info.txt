[DESCRIPTION:Part of the Dwarf Fortress: High Adventure suite. Standalone fork of Succubus Dungeon. The custom weapons are removed - succubi fight with vanilla pikes, halberds, scourges, and whips - and they write on vanilla quires and scrolls, but they keep their full demonic wardrobe: bustiers, corsets, short pants, stockings, and bodysuits. Summer-only caravans (evil trade bloc). Corruption, summoning, powers, magma wells, and everything else carries over unchanged. All IDs renamed HA_SUCCUBUS_* so this fork coexists with the original. See CHANGES.md.]
