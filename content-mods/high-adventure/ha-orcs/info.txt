[NAME:HA - Orcs]
[DESCRIPTION:Part of the Dwarf Fortress: High Adventure suite. Adds warlike orcs: bigger than humans, hostile to every civilization including the evil bloc, living in camp-scale human-style hamlets and conquered settlements, working only pure metals - no alloys, no steel, iron above all. One in five orcs is a champion: a hulking skull-cruncher who feels no pain but flies into frequent rages. Ruled by a primitive warband hierarchy of bosses, war bosses, taskmasters, enforcers, and a great warlord who must be a champion. They never trade and never fear death. Fully playable in fortress mode and adventure mode. See CHANGES.md.]
