graphics_ha_ancient_dragon

[OBJECT:GRAPHICS]

Layered composite (body + tail + both wings + head), each part a LAYER_GROUP whose colour
variants are selected by the scale-tissue colour and coloured by a body-palette row. Every
colour the creature can roll has a matching layer, so no state ever falls back to grayscale
(this is the fix for the grayscale flash). Sprite regions + palette from Naut's Procedural Dragons.
