body_ha_dragon

[OBJECT:BODY]

Ancient dragon anatomy, ported from Naut's Procedural Dragons (body_new.txt).

Plan NAMES carry an HA_ prefix so they cannot collide with the upstream mod if both are
ever installed. The body part TOKENS inside are deliberately left unchanged (HD, NK,
TRHORN, ...) because the creature graphics layers match on part tokens, not plan names --
renaming them would silently break every CONDITION_BP.

Do not write raw tokens in square brackets anywhere in these comments: DF's parser scans
every line for an opening bracket and applies what it finds to the object currently open.

the frame: four legs and four feet, a real NECK-category neck and a HEAD-category
head, which is what the sprite selectors key off
