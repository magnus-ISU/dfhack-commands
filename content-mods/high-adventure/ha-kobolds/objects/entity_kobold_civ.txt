entity_kobold_civ

[OBJECT:ENTITY]

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Design Notes:
	- Civil Kobolds and Skulking Kobolds coexist. They will raid eachother.
	- Kobolds do not have nobility, or trade with their own civilization.
	- They are obligate carnivores and vermin/bone-eaters.
	- Kobold parents have multiple quick-growing children.
	- They're best suited to ranged combat, or using traps and poisons.
	- Kobolds are encouraged to steal goods for use, and to grow their civ.
	- Kobold industry is limited and low-tech, relative to other civs.
	- Kobold industry focuses on wood, bone, leather, cloth, stone, and clay.

Changes:
	- Removed SKULKING and ITEM_THIEF to allow diplomacy/trade.
	- Changed site type to CAVE_DETAILED to let them build hillocks, grow their pop, and be visible on the map.
	- Added domestic venomous snake pets with different uses. Adders for eggs and nonlethal venom, vipers and cobras for meat and rapid-acting dangerous venom, and Helmet Snakes for high-value venomous war pets.
	- Gila monsters and monitor lizards are in as well, for small egg-laying and larger war animals respectively.
	- Can embark with tame cave and phantom spiders, for silk-farming spider pits.
	- Added dance and music forms, plus wind and percussion instruments
	- Adjusted VALUES (see below)

New Content:
	- Shearable Wooly Spiders, which have a weak paralytic bite and limited web-spitting, and are hunting trainable.
	- Made several small tool and weapon equivalents that kobolds can use.
	- Slings, which deal ranged blunt damage, and use thrower/whip skill.
	- Staves, which deal blunt jabs and high-velocity broad swats.
	- Knife-class weapons, which are quick stab-focused blades.

Consider:
	- Set default site to PLAYER_FORTRESS, to create hillocks only? Test in Adventure Mode first.

TODO:
	- When HA_UTTERANCES and a TRANSLATION can coexist, add HA_UTTERANCES back in to kobolds.
