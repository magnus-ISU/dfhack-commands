creature_ha_warlock

[OBJECT:CREATURE]

The civ creature. Four castes, ONE body plan between them -- a caste change that alters body
parts crashes the description view, and the script creates units into these castes, so they
must stay structurally identical. What differs is what they are MADE of.

	WARLOCK_F / WARLOCK_M  the players. Ageless (no MAXAGE), fertile, the only castes that
	                       may hold office or work magic. Flesh, blood, hair and all.
	SKELETON               souls made into labour. Weak, tireless, eats and drinks nothing.
	BONE_GOLEM             10 gold, a gem and many souls. Huge, strong, still a labourer.

The two construct castes are BONE ALL THE WAY THROUGH: they take the vertebrate tissue plan
with BONE substituted for skin, fat and muscle, so a blow that would open a warlock's gut just
scrapes bone, and they have no blood, no hair, no sweat and nothing to infect. Same body parts,
different substance -- which is the safe half of the caste-difference line.

Neither construct carries MALE or FEMALE, so neither can breed, and both sit at POP_RATIO:0 so
worldgen never rolls one; they exist only when a shrine makes them.

OUTSIDER_CONTROLLABLE is creature-level, as it is on every other playable High Adventure race,
so adventurers can be rolled from this civ. The construct castes come along with it -- a
skeleton adventurer is a feature, not an accident.

	========================== THE CONSTRUCTS ==========================
	Bone where a living thing keeps skin, fat and muscle. VERTEBRATE_TISSUE_LAYERS takes its
	five arguments as (skin, fat, muscle, bone, cartilage), so feeding it BONE four times over
	makes every layer of the body the same bone -- no flesh to cut, nothing to bleed, and no
	hair, nails, sweat or infection because none of that is declared for them.
