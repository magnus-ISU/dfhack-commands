creature_ha_prisoner

[OBJECT:CREATURE]

Prisoners: the warlocks' livestock. Bought at embark and from caravans (ANIMAL_TOKEN on the
entity), and dragged home by squads returning from raids (warlocks.lua watches
world.status.mission_reports).

They are deliberately NOT sapient -- no INTELLIGENT, no CAN_LEARN, no CAN_SPEAK -- because
fortress mode refuses to butcher a sapient corpse, and butchering is the whole point. They are
also NOT grazers: only grazers eat, so these are zero-upkeep livestock.

Riots are handled in lua, not raws: the script periodically un-tames a dwarven, human or elven
prisoner, and a wild thing loose in the dungeon is a riot. Goblin and kobold prisoners never do
this -- they have been broken longer.

One creature, five castes, one body plan. The script picks the caste from the race of the site
a raid hit.
