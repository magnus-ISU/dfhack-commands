inorganic_ha_warlock

[OBJECT:INORGANIC]

The soul: this mod's only new material, and its whole currency. Souls are minted by
scripts_modactive/high-adventure/warlocks.lua -- on a kill, and on butchering a prisoner or a
large animal -- and are spent in the shrine, forge and factory reactions.

Note what is deliberately ABSENT: the IS_STONE token. Vanilla writes it explicitly on every
stone (see data/vanilla .../inorganic_other.txt -- STONE_TEMPLATE does not imply it), so
leaving it off gives a well-behaved material that no masonry job, construction, BUILDMAT or
ANY_STONE_MATERIAL reagent will ever accept. Souls appear in no reaction but ours.

The SPECIAL token additionally stops AI civilizations making goods out of it or hauling it
in caravans.
