[OBJECT:GRAPHICS]

Every caste is drawn through a LAYER_SET rather than a plain DEFAULT. That is not decoration:
DF renders a creature in grayscale whenever it flashes one (status or legendary marker) or it
is underwater, and LAYERED graphics are exempt while simple ones flicker grey. Same fix the
ancient golem and the drider already carry.

No item layers anywhere: worn clothing, armour and weapons are only drawn where the graphic
declares a layer for them, and these sprites have no anchor points for that.
