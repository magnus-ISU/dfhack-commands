reaction_ha_high_elf

[OBJECT:REACTION]

High elf reactions -- fully self-contained (own HA_HE_SHAPING_TREE building and
own HA_HE_GROWN wood material; no dependency on HA_playable_civs).

Wood-growing (high elves never cut trees): grow wood/feather wood from seeds at
the Shaping Tree, and make wooden mechanisms at the Craftsdwarf's. Unpaced (no
"wither" cooldown) -- gated only by seed/wood supply.

[REACTION:HA_HE_GROW_WOOD]
	[NAME:grow wood from seed]
	[BUILDING:HA_HE_SHAPING_TREE:CUSTOM_W]
	[REAGENT:A:1:SEEDS:NONE:NONE:NONE][UNROTTEN]
	[PRODUCT:100:1:WOOD:NONE:PLANT_MAT:HA_HE_GROWN:WOOD]
	[SKILL:EXTRACT_STRAND]

[REACTION:HA_HE_GROW_FEATHER]
	[NAME:grow feather tree wood from good seed]
	[BUILDING:HA_HE_SHAPING_TREE:CUSTOM_F]
	[REAGENT:A:1:SEEDS:NONE:PLANT_MAT:BERRY_SUN:SEED][UNROTTEN]
	[PRODUCT:100:1:WOOD:NONE:PLANT_MAT:HA_HE_FEATHER:WOOD]
	[SKILL:EXTRACT_STRAND]

[REACTION:HA_HE_MAKE_WOODEN_MECHANISMS]
	[NAME:make wooden mechanisms]
	[BUILDING:CRAFTSMAN:CUSTOM_SHIFT_K]
	[REAGENT:A:1:WOOD:NONE:NONE:NONE]
	[PRODUCT:100:1:TRAPPARTS:NONE:GET_MATERIAL_FROM_REAGENT:A:NONE]
	[SKILL:WOODCRAFT]

CATCH STARLIGHT (Shaping Tree): 1 plant thread + 1 silk thread -> 1 twinkling
strand (THREAD of HA_TWINKLING_METAL). Weave at a loom into twinkling cloth ->
clothes (vanilla adamantine path -- VERIFY the loom accepts a custom thread-metal
in-game), or forge into a bar below. Strand dimension 15000 = 1 bar / 1 cloth.

NOTE: the twinkling strand is NOT a native [PRODUCT] here. DF hardcodes a fuel
requirement onto any reaction that outputs a *metal* item (twinkling metal is a
METAL_TEMPLATE inorganic), even at a fuel-less custom workshop. To keep starlight-
catching fuel-free, this reaction only consumes its reagents; the high-elves.lua
script hooks HA_CATCH_STARLIGHT and mints the dimension-15000 twinkling THREAD itself.

[REACTION:HA_CATCH_STARLIGHT]
	[NAME:catch starlight]
	[BUILDING:HA_HE_SHAPING_TREE:CUSTOM_S]
	[REAGENT:plant thread:1:THREAD:NO_SUBTYPE:NONE:NONE]
		[ANY_PLANT_MATERIAL]
		[UNROTTEN]
	[REAGENT:silk thread:1:THREAD:NO_SUBTYPE:NONE:NONE]
		[ANY_SILK_MATERIAL]
		[UNROTTEN]
	[SKILL:CLOTHESMAKING]

FORGE TWINKLING BAR: 1 strand + 1 silver bar + fuel -> 1 twinkling metal bar.
[FUEL] = free at a magma smelter, costs coke at a normal one.

[REACTION:HA_FORGE_TWINKLING_BAR]
	[NAME:forge twinkling metal bar]
	[BUILDING:SMELTER:CUSTOM_SHIFT_T]
	[REAGENT:strands:15000:THREAD:NO_SUBTYPE:METAL:HA_TWINKLING_METAL]
	[REAGENT:silver:150:BAR:NO_SUBTYPE:METAL:SILVER]
	[PRODUCT:100:1:BAR:NO_SUBTYPE:METAL:HA_TWINKLING_METAL]
		[PRODUCT_DIMENSION:150]
	[FUEL]
	[SKILL:SMELT]
