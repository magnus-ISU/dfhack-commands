creature_ha_gibberling

Gibberlings: blue, feral goblinoid swarm-beasts of evil lands.
Forked from Highfantasy's "feral goblin" (creature_highfantasy_evil_placeholder,
hordes_GOBLIN_FERAL by Kiiranaux et al), which is itself a berserk copy of the
vanilla goblin body. Re-themed blue, renamed, made self-contained against vanilla,
and set to appear in small swarms. Named for the gibberlings that slew the parents
of Cacame Awemedinade, the Immortal Onslaught.

[OBJECT:CREATURE]
