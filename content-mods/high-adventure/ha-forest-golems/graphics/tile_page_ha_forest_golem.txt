Ancient golem art: the vanilla bronze colossus sprites (creatures_megabeast.png,
its statue sheet and its portrait) recoloured to iron-gray. The PNGs are ours,
extracted and desaturated -- palette recolouring over a vanilla tile page does
not work (palettes remap by colour match, so the vanilla bronze would render
unchanged). Declared PAGE_DIM_PIXELS must match the real PNG exactly or the
compositor reads out of bounds and corrupts the heap.
