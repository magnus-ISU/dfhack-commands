[NAME:HA - Forest Golems]
[DESCRIPTION:Part of the Dwarf Fortress: High Adventure suite. Adds forest golems: ancient iron constructs holding a handful of forest retreats deep in the woods, competing with elves for the same groves. Each civ founds one site and never expands. They have no brain and no skull, so the head and neck are cosmetic; a golden heart buried in the torso is the only thing whose destruction kills them, and it can only be reached by breaking the body open. Their left hand carries a fragile glass flamethrower that is lost for good once shattered. Every golem is legendary in all combat skills except dodging. They wear no armour and no clothing, feel nothing, and neither eat, drink nor sleep. Playable in fort and adventure mode. See README.md and CHANGES.md.]
