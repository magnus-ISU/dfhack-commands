body_ha_forest_golem

[OBJECT:BODY]

Two custom parts for the ancient golem. Both exist so that a body part CATEGORY
can be targeted -- vanilla categories are shared (both hands are CATEGORY:HAND),
and CDI:BP_REQUIRED and TISSUE_LAYER both select by category.

GOLEM_HEART is the kill switch. THOUGHT is the token that makes a part lethal to
lose: destroying a thought centre kills instantly, the same way a brain does.
Putting it in the UPPERBODY rather than the head is what makes the head and neck
cosmetic -- the golem has no brain and no skull, so decapitation takes nothing
vital with it. INTERNAL keeps it buried until the torso is broken open, which is
the "uncover the heart by destroying the body" part.

Vanilla ships an equivalent module (BODY:UB_BRAIN in body_default.txt) but no
vanilla creature uses it, so this path is unproven in play -- see CHANGES.md.

[BODY:HA_GOLEM_HEART]
	[BP:GOLEM_HEART:golden heart:STP][CONTYPE:UPPERBODY][THOUGHT][INTERNAL][SMALL][CATEGORY:GOLEM_HEART]
		[DEFAULT_RELSIZE:100]

FLAMETHROWER is the glass nozzle on the left hand. CON:LH attaches it to the left
hand specifically -- CONTYPE:GRASP would attach one to each hand and give the
golem two of them. It is a separate part rather than a re-tissued hand so that
CDI:BP_REQUIRED can name it: lose the nozzle (or the hand carrying it, which
takes its children with it) and the flame interaction is revoked.

Small on purpose: a low RELSIZE makes it an easy part to sever, which is the
"fragile" half of the design.

[BODY:HA_GOLEM_FLAMETHROWER]
	[BP:FLAMETHROWER:flamethrower:STP][CON:LH][SMALL][CATEGORY:FLAMETHROWER]
		[DEFAULT_RELSIZE:30]
