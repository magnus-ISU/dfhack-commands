creature_ha_forest_golem

[OBJECT:CREATURE]

Forest golem: an iron construct that lives in forest retreats deep in the woods.
Built from the vanilla bronze colossus's physical model (one metal tissue over
every part, no organs, no blood, no bones) but INTELLIGENT and civ-capable, so
they hold sites, take professions and can be played in fort and adventure mode.

Three things make them fight differently to anything else in the suite:

  * IRON BODY, NO VITAL ORGANS IN THE HEAD. There is no brain and no skull, so
    severing the neck or crushing the head accomplishes nothing.
  * A GOLDEN HEART in the upper body carries the THOUGHT token. It is the only
    part whose destruction kills, and INTERNAL keeps it behind the iron until
    the torso is broken open.
  * A GLASS FLAMETHROWER on the left hand, gated by CDI:BP_REQUIRED. Shatter the
    nozzle and the flame is gone for the rest of the fight.

NOEMOTION is deliberate: they are constructs, they do not feel, and a fort of
them never tantrums. NOTHOUGHT is deliberately NOT set -- that would empty the
thoughts/needs panels a playable civ is built around.
