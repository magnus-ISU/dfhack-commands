[NAME:HA - Illithids]
[DESCRIPTION:Part of the Dwarf Fortress: High Adventure suite. Adds the illithids: ageless, sexless psionic brain-eaters in isolated dark fortresses, hostile to all and trading with none, unable to wear armor but robed and terrible. Castes: illithids (psionic mind blasts, legendary paralytic bite), ulitharids (iron-hard skin, no hunger), the vast fragile Elder Brain (ten grasping tentacles, the colony's god), and human thralls who breed so the colony may grow. Their Neural Bath devours the brains of sentients (with a chance of ascension to a ulitharid), reclaims dead illithids' memories, extracts precious ulitharid brains, implants tadpoles in prisoners' skulls, coalesces a new Elder Brain from a preserved ulitharid brain and adamantine thread, and lets the Elder Brain resonate with the colony. Ten psionic levels scale with scholarly mastery. See CHANGES.md.]
