creature_ha_summon_test

[OBJECT:CREATURE]

Test-only vanilla edit (this mod is never shipped): dwarves cast the summons on
greeting, so an ordinary embark produces summons within a few hundred ticks of
social contact. Dog summons are frequent, dwarf summons rarer.

[SELECT_CREATURE:DWARF]
	[CAN_DO_INTERACTION:HA_TEST_SUMMON_DOG]
		[CDI:ADV_NAME:Summon test dog]
		[CDI:VERB:conjure a test dog:conjures a test dog:NA]
		[CDI:USAGE_HINT:GREETING]
		[CDI:TARGET:A:SELF_ONLY]
		[CDI:WAIT_PERIOD:400]
	[CAN_DO_INTERACTION:HA_TEST_SUMMON_DWARF]
		[CDI:ADV_NAME:Summon test dwarf]
		[CDI:VERB:conjure a test dwarf:conjures a test dwarf:NA]
		[CDI:USAGE_HINT:GREETING]
		[CDI:TARGET:A:SELF_ONLY]
		[CDI:WAIT_PERIOD:1200]
