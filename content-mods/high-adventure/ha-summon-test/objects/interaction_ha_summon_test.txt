interaction_ha_summon_test

[OBJECT:INTERACTION]

Permanent-summon test: modeled on the succubus SPIT_SOUL interactions but with
IE_TIME_RANGE deliberately omitted (vanilla shrine blessings omit it and their
summoned pets are permanent).

[INTERACTION:HA_TEST_SUMMON_DOG]
	[I_SOURCE:CREATURE_ACTION]
	[I_TARGET:A:LOCATION]
		[IT_LOCATION:CONTEXT_LOCATION]
	[I_TARGET:B:LOCATION]
		[IT_LOCATION:RANDOM_NEARBY_LOCATION:A:2]
	[I_EFFECT:SUMMON_UNIT]
		[IE_TARGET:B]
		[IE_IMMEDIATE]
		[CREATURE:DOG:DEFAULT]
		[IE_MAKE_PET_IF_POSSIBLE]

The sapient case, which is what the breeding pit / ceremorphosis actually needs.
Note the vanilla blessing generator forbids CAN_LEARN summons - citizenship of a
summoned sapient is exactly what this probes.
