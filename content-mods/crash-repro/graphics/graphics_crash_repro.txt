graphics_crash_repro

THIS FILE IS THE BUG. Everything else in this mod is scaffolding to get the
creature on screen.

body.png is 64x32 pixels: two 32x32 tiles side by side, magenta then cyan. The
tile page below declares the page to be 32x32 -- ONE tile, the magenta one. The
cyan tile is real image data that the declaration does not admit exists.

The layer then asks for tile 1:0 -- the cyan one. It is inside the file and
outside the declared grid. DF neither rejects the reference at load nor clamps
it at draw time: the layered-sprite compositor indexes past the end of the
surface it allocated from the declared dimensions and writes out of bounds,
corrupting the heap. The game then aborts or segfaults seconds to minutes
later, wherever the next allocation happens to land.

Change PAGE_DIM_PIXELS to 64:32 and the crashes stop.

[OBJECT:GRAPHICS]

[TILE_PAGE:CRASH_REPRO_PAGE]
	[FILE:crash_repro/body.png]
	[TILE_DIM:32:32]
	[PAGE_DIM_PIXELS:32:32]

[CREATURE_GRAPHICS:CRASH_REPRO]
	[LAYER_SET:DEFAULT]
	[LAYER_GROUP]
	[LAYER:BODY_OUT_OF_BOUNDS:CRASH_REPRO_PAGE:1:0]
	[END_LAYER_GROUP]
