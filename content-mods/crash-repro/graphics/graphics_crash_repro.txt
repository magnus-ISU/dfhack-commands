graphics_crash_repro

THIS FILE IS THE BUG. Everything else in this mod is scaffolding to get the
creature on screen.

body.png is 256x32 pixels: EIGHT 32x32 tiles in a row. The tile page below
declares the page to be 32x32 -- ONE tile, tile 0. Tiles 1..7 are real image
data that the declaration does not admit exists.

The layers below then ask for tiles 1:0 and 7:0 -- inside the file, outside the
declared grid -- and each one is recoloured through a palette. The palette is
the part that matters: a plain layer just blits an existing page texture and
SDL clips the read harmlessly (measured: a no-palette version of this mod did
NOT crash). A palette layer makes DF build a NEW recoloured copy of the tile --
that is the textures::create_texture / SDL_CreateRGBSurface allocation valgrind
catches being written past. Every one of the six layers in the mod that first
exposed this bug (ha-succubi) was a palette layer.

Three USE_PALETTE indices mean three separate recoloured copies, so the fault
is hit three times per creature rather than once.

Change PAGE_DIM_PIXELS to 256:32 and the crashes stop.

[OBJECT:GRAPHICS]

[TILE_PAGE:CRASH_REPRO_PAGE]
	[FILE:crash_repro/body.png]
	[TILE_DIM:32:32]
	[PAGE_DIM_PIXELS:32:32]

[CREATURE_GRAPHICS:CRASH_REPRO]
	[LAYER_SET:DEFAULT]

	[LS_PALETTE:BODY]
		[LS_PALETTE_FILE:crash_repro/palettes.png]
		[LS_PALETTE_DEFAULT:0]

	body

		[LAYER_GROUP]
		[LAYER:BODY_IN_BOUNDS:CRASH_REPRO_PAGE:0:0]
			[USE_PALETTE:BODY:1]
		[END_LAYER_GROUP]

	one tile past the declared edge -- the exact shape of the ha-succubi bug

		[LAYER_GROUP]
		[LAYER:BODY_OUT_OF_BOUNDS_NEAR:CRASH_REPRO_PAGE:1:0]
			[USE_PALETTE:BODY:2]
		[END_LAYER_GROUP]

	seven tiles past it -- a bigger overrun, in case one tile lands inside
	whatever slack the allocator happened to leave

		[LAYER_GROUP]
		[LAYER:BODY_OUT_OF_BOUNDS_FAR:CRASH_REPRO_PAGE:7:0]
			[USE_PALETTE:BODY:3]
		[END_LAYER_GROUP]
