Burrow Stamper Sandbox

Throwaway packer prototype. Roads grow from the entry one segment at a time; each segment is dressed and lined with districts as it is laid.

fort/burrow-stamper

Sandbox controls (not part of the in-game UI)

Changing the preset resets road widths to the preset's values. Margin, caps, shared walls and setback are per-district fields in the JSON. "Existing geometry" generates a smaller preset on a central patch first; the main preset then grows around it and may join its roads.

The algorithm

1. A road head is a tile, a direction and a width; the first one sits on the entry pointing inward, main-road width. Heads are processed breadth-first. 2. A segment advances tile by tile: its full-width footprint must be unclaimed free rock, and the tiles just beyond both edges may not be road, so roads never lie side by side or touch at a corner. It ends at its wanted length, at rock, or one tile short of an existing road it meets across its full width, which makes a T junction and a loop. Every segment ends in a full square cap, so a turn is two rectangles meeting at a square: corners are valid by construction. 3. The segment is dressed at once: a statue row rolled into its middle if long enough, a plaza rolled onto the junction square before anything else claims it. Statues are blocked road, so no door ever faces one. 4. Each side of the segment is a sequence of districts chosen by quota: a repeat district lines the road with its stamps, doors on the road, until its capped stamps are used or its length allowance is spent; a set district is a suite, hub door on the road, other rooms attached wall to wall growing square. Stubs for side streets are reserved first so rooms leave the gap. 5. At the cap the segment spawns straight, turn, T or cross heads by width and depth, and side streets also branch mid-segment. Segments left with no rooms and no children are removed. 6. Room floors smooth, room walls engrave, road floors smooth, rock beside a road that no room owns smooth.
